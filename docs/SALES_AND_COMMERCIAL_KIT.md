## 2. Core Positioning & One-Page Executive Overview

### Headline Positioning
**Aesthetic Revenue OS** is the cross-location revenue intelligence and recovery layer for **5–50+ location medspa and aesthetics groups**. We connect your existing non-clinical POS and CRM exports in 48 hours, surface exact clinic-by-clinic revenue leaks across lead response, consultation conversion, repeat-visit retention, and memberships, coordinate human-approved follow-up, and verify every recovered dollar against settled POS receipts.

### One-Page Executive Overview (Send Ahead of Calls)
- **The Multi-Location Blind Spot:** Boulevard ($158–$468/mo/site), Zenoti ($300–$600+/mo/site), and Aesthetic Record handle single-clinic scheduling and charting. Yet as groups scale past 5–8 locations (especially via PE tuck-ins), leadership cannot see in one place which studios take 9 hours to answer inquiries, where attended consultations fail to convert, or which studios have hundreds of 110+ day single-visit drop-offs.
- **Why It Costs Six Figures:** The 2026 AmSpa / CorralData 100+ brand benchmark showed median revenue grew 19.2% YoY on just 1.2% new-patient growth—driven by repeat wallet share ($1,681 repeat vs. $355 single-visit) and memberships (2.5x spend, +35 pts retention), while <15-minute lead response separated top-half growers from laggards.
- **How Aesthetic Revenue OS Works (Zero EMR Rip-and-Replace):**
  1. **Ingest:** Upload standard non-clinical POS/CRM CSV exports (zero patient charts or medical histories).
  2. **Detect:** Evidence-backed Opportunity Inbox pinpoints exact uncontacted leads, stalled consultations, 110+ day inactive guests, and membership gaps by studio.
  3. **Execute Safely:** Front-desk/central concierge teams review and approve consent-verified follow-ups in one click (zero autonomous rogue messaging).
  4. **Verify ROI:** Our 6-stage Attribution Ledger strictly separates *Identified Pipeline* from *POS-Receipt-Verified Collected Revenue*.
